# StateLoom visual system

The supplied logo is a soft 3D infrastructure mark: three stacked storage slabs and
a verification shield. Its strongest colors are electric blue `#1877F2`, sky
`#42B4FF`, indigo `#4038D5`, midnight `#0B2347`, mint `#18D99A`, and teal `#00969D`.
The white field and broad rounded geometry communicate safety, but the product UI
will deliberately contrast it with a dark forensic workbench.

- Direction: industrial/editorial control room, not a card dashboard.
- Display face: Georgia-style high-contrast serif; operational text: monospace.
- Composition: asymmetric 12-column rail, dense storage rows, generous outer frame.
- Radius: 2px for data surfaces, 999px only for state capsules.
- Motion: 420ms rail reveal; restrained scan line; no decorative bouncing.
- Accessibility: body contrast >= 4.5:1, visible focus rings, 44px controls.
- Memorable device: a vertical illuminated "slot spine" connecting old and candidate state.
